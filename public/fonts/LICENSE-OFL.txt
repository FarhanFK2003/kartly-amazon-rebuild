Kartly bundled typefaces
========================

Both files in this directory are redistributed under the SIL Open Font License,
Version 1.1, which permits bundling and redistribution — including inside a
commercial or public repository — provided the fonts are not sold on their own
and this notice travels with them.

They are committed rather than fetched so that neither a build nor a page view
depends on a third-party host. This is the same rule the product imagery follows
(see ATTRIBUTION.md for that chain).
